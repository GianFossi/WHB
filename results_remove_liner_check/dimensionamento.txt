12. DIMENSIONAMENTO AUTOMATICO
================================================================================================
  Questa sezione usa il risultato del calcolo completo per proporre il dimensionamento
  automatico. L'ordine e' intenzionale: prima vincoli PDS, poi margini
  termici/idraulici/vibrazionali, poi minimizzazione di peso WHB e lunghezza tubi.

  VINCOLI E VERIFICHE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  OBIETTIVO DI OTTIMIZZAZIONE
  Peso WHB stimato                                   71.6 t
  Lunghezza tubi                                     13.00 m
  Altezza centerline drum-WHB                        6.00 m (min screened 3.33 m)
  Nozzle / spool raiser minimo                       200 / 500 mm
  Peso riser + downcomer                             47.35 t (13.5 + 67.4 m sviluppati)
  WHB ID x L / drum ID x L                           26.32 / 39.00 m2
  Objective hierarchy: satisfy PDS first, then safety margins, then minimize WHB metal
  weight, tube length, steam-drum elevation, riser/downcomer weight, and WHB/drum ID x L
  envelopes. Preferred levers are low-weight/no-length changes before larger bundle
  geometry.
  Minimize the steam-drum centerline elevation against the WHB centerline after allowing
  WHB OD, drum OD, nozzle height, and minimum riser spool. Then minimize external piping
  weight and the ID x L envelopes of WHB and steam drum.

  AZIONI DI DIMENSIONAMENTO PROPOSTE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Heat flux / DNBR
    Stato attuale                                    q''max 396 kW/m2, DNBR 0.72
    Richiesto                                        Area factor about 2.78, or extend protected inlet length to about 759 mm.
    Beneficio                                        Keeps peak flux below the limit and raises DNBR.
    Impatto peso/lunghezza                           Ferrules add little weight and no tube length; more area adds weight or length.
    Applicabile all'esistente                        SI
    The weight/length objective prefers ferrule/protection changes first, then extra tubes,
    and only last additional tube length.

  Steam-drum elevation
    Stato attuale                                    centerline difference 6.00 m; minimum screened value 3.33 m
    Richiesto                                        Lower the steam drum toward 3.33 m while rechecking circulation, nozzle geometry, and riser spool clearance.
    Beneficio                                        Reduces riser/downcomer developed length, piping weight, support steel, and static elevation.
    Impatto peso/lunghezza                           Current riser/downcomer metal estimate is 47.35 t over 80.9 m developed length.
    Applicabile all'esistente                        NO / solo nuovo fascio
    The lower bound includes WHB OD, steam-drum OD, default 200 mm nozzle height, and the
    minimum riser spool criterion.

  Envelope compactness
    Stato attuale                                    WHB ID x L 26.32 m2; drum ID x L 39.00 m2
    Richiesto                                        Minimize both products only after PDS duty, generated steam, gas outlet temperature, gas dP, DNBR, heat flux, circulation, and vibration checks remain satisfied.
    Beneficio                                        Keeps WHB and steam-drum envelopes compact.
    Impatto peso/lunghezza                           Smaller ID x L generally lowers metal weight, external piping length, insulation, platforms, and plot impact.
    Applicabile all'esistente                        NO / solo nuovo fascio
    This is an optimization objective, not a standalone pass/fail constraint.

  Vibration
    Stato attuale                                    V/Vcrit 4.14 at span 1290 mm
    Richiesto                                        22 baffles, uniform span about 546 mm
    Beneficio                                        Avoids fluid-elastic instability problems.
    Impatto peso/lunghezza                           Adds baffle weight but does not increase tube length.
    Applicabile all'esistente                        NO / solo nuovo fascio
    For an existing bundle this is usually not practical; for a new unit it is the preferred
    fix.
